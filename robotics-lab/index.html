    01Workbench

    Wire it up on the workbench

    Put a board on the bench, add sensors, motors and displays, run jumper wires from pin to pin and press Run. Guided missions walk you through real circuits; Free build lets you wire anything.

    • Missions

      Step-by-step builds — blink an LED, read a sensor, drive a motor.

    • Real wiring

      Click a pin, click another: the bench checks every connection before power-up.

    • Room to work

      Press Full screen in the workbench toolbar to give the bench the whole display.

    The workbench is still being set up. The boards, lessons and quiz are ready in the meantime.

    03Quiz & assembly

    Now try it with your own hands

    Reading about a board is one thing; finding the parts yourself is what makes it stick. Both exercises run on the 3D model at the top of the page and work for every board.

    Quiz · about a minute

    Find the part

    We name a part — you find it on the model and click it. Rotate the board, zoom in and flip it over, just like the real thing.

    1. 1

      Read the question. “Find the microcontroller — the brain of the board.”

    2. 2

      Click the part. Not sure? Take a hint, or skip and see the answer.

    3. 3

      See your score. Only first-try answers without a hint count.

    Assembly · a few minutes

    Take it apart, put it back together

    The board comes apart into a tray of parts on the bench. Put every chip, connector and header back where it belongs and solder it down.

    1. 1

      Pick a part from the tray. Chips, connectors, headers, buttons.

    2. 2

      Place it on its footprint. The pads on the bare board show where it goes.

    3. 3

      Solder it in. When the last joint is done, the board is whole again.
